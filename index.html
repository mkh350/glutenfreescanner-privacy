🌾 Gluten Free Scanner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

Summary: Gluten Free Scanner processes all data on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

1. What the App Does

Gluten Free Scanner helps users identify gluten in food products by scanning ingredient lists (using the device camera) and looking up barcodes (using the Open Food Facts public database). The app provides a gluten status assessment based on detected ingredients, allergen tags, and E-number classification data sourced from Coeliac UK and EFSA guidelines.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.
Feature Data Used Where Processed Stored or Sent?
Camera (OCR scan) Camera images of food labels On your device only Never stored or transmitted. Images are processed in memory and immediately discarded.
Barcode scan Barcode number On your device + Open Food Facts API The barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product information. No personal data is included in this request.
Scan history Previous scan results On your device only Stored locally in the app's database on your device. Never transmitted to any server. You can clear history at any time from the app.
Advertisements Device advertising identifier Google AdMob Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's privacy policy below.

3. Camera Permission

The app requires camera access to scan food labels and barcodes. The camera is used exclusively for this purpose. No photos are saved to your device, uploaded to any server, or shared with any third party.

4. Internet Permission

The app uses internet access for two purposes only:

  • Barcode lookup: To query the Open Food Facts public database (openfoodfacts.org) for product information.
  • Advertisements: To load ads served by Google AdMob.

No personal information, device data, or scan results are transmitted over the internet by the app itself.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Data Security

All scan data is processed and stored locally on your device. Since we do not collect or transmit personal data, there is no risk of data breach from our side. We recommend keeping your device software up to date for optimal security.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. Your scan history is stored locally and can be cleared at any time from within the app.

9. Medical Disclaimer

Gluten Free Scanner is a tool to assist in identifying gluten in food products. It is not a medical device and should not be used as the sole basis for dietary decisions. Always read product labels carefully and consult with a healthcare professional if you have celiac disease or gluten sensitivity. The E-number classification data is based on Coeliac UK and EFSA guidelines but may not reflect the most current information.
